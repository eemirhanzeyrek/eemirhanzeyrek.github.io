Highlights

1,200+ Curated CEFR Words

Structured vocabulary across all 6 CEFR levels (A1 to C2) with phonetic guides, definitions, and example sentences—plus 200 free essential words.

Dynamic Practice Modes

Train with Smart Flashcards, Survival and Sprint quiz runs, flexible pace options (4s to Untimed), and dedicated “Weak Words” reinforcement.

100% Offline & Native Audio

Study anywhere without internet. Word pronunciations are synthesized directly on-device via Apple’s native AVSpeechSynthesizer.

Zero Data Collection

No account required, no analytics SDKs, and zero tracking. Your quiz history, streaks, and mastery records stay locally on your iPhone via SwiftData.

Private iCloud Sync

Optionally sync mastered words, quiz history, streaks, and saved favorites across devices through your encrypted personal Apple iCloud container.

Local Reminders & JSON Export

Customizable on-device study reminders via UserNotifications without push servers, plus instant JSON backup export anytime.
