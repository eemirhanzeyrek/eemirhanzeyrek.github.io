Vonimo: Vocabulary Builder

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

At Vonimo, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Vonimo is engineered from the ground up to operate with zero surveillance and zero data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Account Required

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal, educational, or behavioral data.
  • You do not need to register, provide an email, or create an account to use Vonimo.
  • We have no knowledge of what words you study, your quiz scores, your study streaks, or your app usage habits.

2. On-Device Storage & Local Data Privacy

  • Local Storage: All vocabulary mastery levels, test logs, learning streaks, and bookmarks reside strictly on your device via Apple SwiftData.
  • Zero Cloud Transmission: We operate no external databases or cloud servers. Your learning data never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup file via the iOS share sheet. Nobody—including the developer—has access to your learning records.

3. No Third-Party Tracking & No Analytics SDKs

  • Vonimo contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs (e.g., no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK).
  • In accordance with Apple’s Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), NSPrivacyTracking is set to false, and no tracking domains or collected data types are utilized. Only standard local user defaults are accessed for app preferences (daily goals, theme, audio).

4. In-App Purchases & Payment Security

  • All transactions are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2.
  • We never see, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details. Payment processing is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. On-Device Audio & Local Notifications

  • Word pronunciations are synthesized directly on your device using Apple’s native AVSpeechSynthesizer. Vonimo does not request microphone permissions and does not record or analyze audio.
  • Daily study reminders are scheduled strictly on your local device via Apple’s UserNotifications framework without external push servers.

6. Children’s Privacy

Vonimo does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update our Privacy Policy if new features are added. However, our core commitment to zero data collection remains permanent. If you have any privacy-related inquiries, contact:
