Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my Pro Lifetime purchase?

You can restore your purchase at any time on any device linked to your Apple ID:

  1. Open Vonimo.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (gear icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap “Restore Purchases” (or use the Restore button on the upgrade screen).

Your purchase will be instantly re-verified with Apple’s StoreKit 2 at zero additional cost.

2. Does Vonimo work completely offline?

Yes. All 1,260+ curated vocabulary words, phonetic guides, definitions, and learning histories are stored directly on your iPhone. Pronunciation uses Apple’s native on-device speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer), meaning you can study, listen, and quiz yourself with zero internet connection or cellular data.

3. How does the free version (Freemium) work?

  • Free Tier: Instant, unrestricted access to the first 50 A1 and first 50 A2 words (100 core foundational words), full Sprint quiz mode, and 1 daily Survival mode challenge.
  • Vonimo Pro (One-Time Lifetime Purchase): Unlocks all 1,260+ words across all 6 CEFR levels (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2), unlimited Survival runs, dedicated “Weak Words” mistake review, and the Custom Practice builder. No recurring subscriptions, no hidden fees.

4. How can I back up or transfer my study progress?

Go to Settings → Export Data. Vonimo generates a local, human-readable backup file (Vonimo_Backup_*.json) that you can save to the Files app, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop to another device via the native iOS share sheet.

5. Can I delete all my learning data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Reset Progress. This instantly and permanently erases all quiz records, review logs, streaks, and saved favorites from your device’s local SwiftData storage.

Privacy Policy

At Vonimo, we believe that language learning should be an entirely personal and private experience. Our architecture is built on strict privacy-by-design principles:

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, harvest, monitor, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No account registration, email sign-up, or login is ever required.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or advertising IDs (IDFA).
  • We have zero visibility into what words you study, your test scores, or your app usage habits.

2. 100% On-Device Processing & Storage

All user data—including study streaks, XP, mastered words, test logs, and bookmarks—is stored solely on your physical device using Apple’s local SwiftData framework. We do not own, operate, or communicate with any cloud databases or external remote servers.

3. No Advertising & No Third-Party SDKs

Vonimo contains:

  • Zero third-party ad networks or tracking banners.
  • Zero third-party analytics SDKs (no Google Firebase, no Meta SDK, no telemetry brokers).
  • Zero cross-app tracking.

4. Apple System Frameworks & Security

  • In-App Purchases: All transactions for Vonimo Pro are handled directly and securely by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never see, receive, or store your credit card or billing details.
  • Audio Pronunciation: Handled strictly on-device using Apple’s native AVFoundation framework. No microphone permissions are requested, and no audio data is recorded or transmitted.
  • Local Notifications: Daily study reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device via Apple’s UserNotifications framework without any push notification servers.
