Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does Vonimo Pro work?

Vonimo provides free foundational access across A1 and A2 CEFR levels. Vonimo Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase (“Pay once, own forever”). There are no recurring subscription fees or auto-renewals for the app. Upgrading unlocks all 1,200+ curated words across all 6 CEFR levels (A1 through C2), unlimited Survival mode runs, custom practice sessions, and dedicated “Weak Words” error reinforcement.

2. How do I restore my Vonimo Pro purchase?

If you reinstall the app or set up a new Apple device:

  1. Open Vonimo and go to Settings (or tap the Vonimo Pro banner).
  2. Tap “Restore Purchases”.
  3. Apple StoreKit 2 will securely validate your previous purchase against your Apple ID and unlock your Pro access instantly.

3. Does Vonimo work completely offline?

Yes. All 1,200+ curated vocabulary words, phonetic guides, definitions, and learning histories are stored directly on your iPhone. Pronunciation uses Apple’s native on-device speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer), meaning you can study, listen, and quiz yourself with zero internet connection or cellular data.

4. Where is my vocabulary and learning progress stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple’s native SwiftData framework. We do not operate remote servers, cloud databases, or have access to your test scores, study streaks, or learning records.

5. How do study reminders work?

Reminders are scheduled entirely on your local device using Apple’s UserNotifications framework. Vonimo does not use external push servers.

6. How can I export or permanently delete my data?

  • Export: Go to Settings > Data > Export Learning Data to generate a standard backup file (JSON) that you can save to the Files app, iCloud Drive, or transfer via AirDrop.
  • Delete: Go to Settings > Data > Reset Progress to permanently erase all quiz records, review logs, streaks, and saved favorites from your device.
