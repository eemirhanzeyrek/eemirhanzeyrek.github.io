Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does Mecofi Pro work?

Mecofi Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase (“Pay once, own forever”). There are no recurring subscription fees, hidden charges, or auto-renewals. Upgrading unlocks:

  • Unlimited Document Scanner: Scan multi-page documents and receipts without the 3-page limit.
  • Unlimited Photos to PDF: Convert unlimited photos into clean, consolidated PDF files.
  • Batch Image Conversion: Convert multiple images simultaneously with no batch caps.
  • Next-Gen Formats: Full support for modern HEIC and WebP conversion alongside JPEG and PNG.
  • Video Compression: Shrink video file sizes with presets for Sharing, 720p HD, and 1080p Full HD.
  • Biometric Vault Lock: Secure your private files and processed media with Face ID / Touch ID.
  • Private iCloud Sync: Keep your processed files and metadata seamlessly synced across your Apple devices.
  • Complete ZIP Vault Export: Back up or export your entire Vault archive in a single tap.

2. How do I restore my Mecofi Pro purchase?

If you reinstall the app or set up a new Apple device:

  1. Open Mecofi and tap the Settings tab.
  2. Tap the Mecofi Pro banner (or access the Paywall sheet).
  3. Tap “Restore Purchases” at the top or bottom of the screen.

Apple StoreKit 2 will securely validate your previous transaction against your Apple ID and restore your lifetime access immediately without recharging you.

3. Does Mecofi require an internet connection or upload my files to the cloud?

No. Mecofi is engineered with a 100% offline, on-device architecture:

  • All conversions (photos, PDFs, documents, audio extraction, video compression) run locally using Apple’s high-performance native frameworks (AVFoundation, Core Image, ImageIO, and VisionKit).
  • Your documents, photos, audio files, and videos never leave your device.
  • We do not operate external processing servers, cloud transcoding APIs, or third-party tracking databases.

4. Where are my converted and scanned files stored?

All processed files are securely stored inside your device’s isolated local app sandbox (accessible through the in-app Vault tab).

  • If you are a Pro user and choose to enable iCloud Sync, your data is encrypted and synced exclusively through your personal, private Apple CloudKit container tied to your own Apple ID.
  • Nobody—including the developer—has access to your documents, photos, or media files.

5. What file formats does Mecofi support?

  • Images: Convert between HEIC, JPEG, PNG, and WebP with customizable compression quality.
  • Documents & Scans: Apple VisionKit-powered multi-page scanner with automatic perspective correction, document edge detection, and PDF generation.
  • Media & Audio: Extract crystal-clear audio tracks (M4A) from videos, or compress MP4/MOV videos with customizable bitrate presets.

6. How can I export or permanently delete my data?

  • Export Vault Data: Go to Settings > Data & Sync > Export Vault Data to package all your stored files into an uncompressed or standard .zip archive ready to share via AirDrop, Files, or Mail.
  • Delete All Data: Go to Settings > Data & Sync > Delete All Data to permanently erase all locally stored files, scanned documents, thumbnails, and database records from your device in one action.
