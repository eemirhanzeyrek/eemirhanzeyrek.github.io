Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does Mecofi Pro work?

Mecofi Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase (“Pay once, own forever”). There are no recurring subscription fees or auto-renewals. Upgrading unlocks unlimited batch file conversions, high-resolution document scanning, custom PDF page compilations, full audio format extractions, and future conversion tools.

2. How do I restore my Mecofi Pro purchase?

If you reinstall the app or set up a new Apple device:

  1. Open Mecofi and go to Settings (or tap the Mecofi Pro banner).
  2. Tap “Restore Purchases”.
  3. Apple StoreKit 2 will securely validate your previous purchase against your Apple ID and unlock your Pro access instantly.

3. Do my files, photos, videos, or scans ever leave my device?

Never. Mecofi is engineered with a strict 100% on-device architecture. All document scans, image conversions, video audio extractions, and PDF compilations are executed entirely locally on your iPhone using native Apple system frameworks (VisionKit, PDFKit, AVFoundation). Your files never touch external servers or cloud services.

4. Does Mecofi require an active internet connection?

No. All core conversion utilities operate completely offline. An internet connection is only accessed by iOS when verifying in-app purchases or restoring purchases through Apple’s App Store servers.

5. What file formats does Mecofi support?

  • Documents & Scanning: Multi-page PDF creation, smart document scanning, auto-edge detection, and perspective correction.
  • Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF, GIF, BMP, and PDF.
  • Media & Audio: Video-to-audio extraction and conversion supporting MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, and CAF from MP4, MOV, and M4V video files.

6. Where are my converted files stored and how do I delete them?

  • Storage: All converted files are stored locally in your device’s sandboxed Vault. You can export them to the iOS Files app or share them via AirDrop.
  • Delete: You can delete any converted file from within the Vault, or delete the app to permanently erase all locally stored data.
