Highlights

Next-Gen Image Conversion

Convert between HEIC, JPEG, PNG, and WebP with customizable compression quality and fast batch processing.

Smart Document Scanner & PDF

VisionKit-powered multi-page scanner with automatic edge detection, perspective correction, and clean Photos-to-PDF creation.

Video Compression & Audio

Shrink MP4 and MOV videos with presets (Sharing, 720p HD, 1080p Full HD) or extract crystal-clear M4A audio tracks.

100% Offline & On-Device

Every conversion runs locally via AVFoundation, Core Image, and ImageIO. Your files never leave your device—zero cloud servers.

Biometric Local Vault

Store processed media and scanned documents in an isolated app sandbox protected by native Face ID or Touch ID lock.

Private iCloud Sync & ZIP Export

Optionally sync your Vault through your personal encrypted CloudKit container, or back up everything as a .zip archive in one tap.
