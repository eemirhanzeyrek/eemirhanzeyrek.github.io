Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my lifetime Pro purchase?

Open Mecofi, open Settings, and tap “Restore Purchases”. Your lifetime access will be verified instantly via Apple StoreKit.

Do my files, photos, or scans ever leave my device?

Never. Mecofi processes document scanning, image format conversions, and audio extraction 100% locally on your iPhone using native Apple system frameworks.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Zero Data Collection: Mecofi does not collect, log, track, or share any personal documents, scanned files, or user identity records.

2. 100% On-Device Processing: All file conversions and scans are computed strictly using native Apple frameworks without communicating with cloud servers.

3. Zero Third-Party SDKs: No third-party trackers, analytics, or advertising frameworks are bundled in the application.

4. Secure Purchases: All in-app transactions are handled directly and securely through Apple’s native StoreKit.
