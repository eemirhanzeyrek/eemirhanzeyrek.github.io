Mecofi: File & Media Converter

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

At Mecofi, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Mecofi is engineered from the ground up to operate with zero surveillance, zero cloud dependencies, and zero data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Account Required

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, monitor, or sell any personal data, media files, or behavioral telemetry.
  • You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create an account to use Mecofi.
  • We have no knowledge of the files you import, convert, scan, compress, or store inside your private Vault.

2. 100% On-Device Processing & Private iCloud Sync

  • Local Processing & Storage: All image conversions, document scans, PDF creations, audio extractions, and video compressions are executed strictly on your physical device using Apple’s native frameworks (AVFoundation, Core Image, ImageIO, VisionKit, and SwiftData). Your files are never uploaded to external servers or cloud transcoding services.
  • Isolated Vault: Processed files reside exclusively within your device’s isolated application sandbox directory.
  • iCloud Sync: If enabled (Mecofi Pro), sync operations occur directly between your device and your personal Apple iCloud account via Apple’s CloudKit Private Database. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Nobody—including the developer—has access to your iCloud containers or files.

3. Device Permissions (Camera & Photo Library)

Mecofi requests system permissions only when strictly necessary to deliver on-device functionality:

  • Camera Access: Used solely to scan documents, papers, and receipts through Apple’s native Document Camera (VisionKit). Scanned frames are processed entirely on-device and are never recorded or transmitted outside your device.
  • Photos & Media Library Access: Used solely to let you choose photos and videos for conversion or to save converted media directly to your Photos library upon your explicit action.

4. No Third-Party Tracking & No Analytics SDKs

  • Mecofi contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, tracking libraries, or remote analytics SDKs (e.g., no Google Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Meta/Facebook SDK, no crash-reporting trackers).
  • In accordance with Apple’s Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), NSPrivacyTracking is set to false, and no tracking domains or collected data types are utilized.
  • Only standard local device preferences (UserDefaults) are accessed for basic app settings (such as appearance mode and language selection).

5. In-App Purchases & Payment Security

  • All financial transactions (including Mecofi Pro lifetime unlock) are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2.
  • We never see, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking information. All payment processing is strictly governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Biometric Security (Face ID / Touch ID)

  • If you enable the Vault Lock feature, authentication is performed locally on your device using Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework and the Secure Enclave.
  • Mecofi never has access to your biometric samples, facial scans, fingerprint data, or cryptographic biometric keys.

7. Children’s Privacy

Mecofi does not collect or solicit any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for all audiences.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update our Privacy Policy if new offline utilities or features are added. However, our fundamental commitment to 100% on-device processing and zero data collection remains permanent.
