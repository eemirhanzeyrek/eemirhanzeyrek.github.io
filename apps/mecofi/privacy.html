Mecofi: File & Media Converter

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

At Mecofi, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Mecofi is engineered from the ground up to operate with zero surveillance and zero data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Account Required

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal, document, image, or audio conversion data.
  • You do not need to register, provide an email, or create an account to use Mecofi.
  • We have no knowledge of the files you scan, convert, or compile into PDFs.

2. On-Device Storage & Local Processing

  • Local Processing: All computations are executed strictly on your device using native Apple system frameworks (VisionKit, PDFKit, AVFoundation).
  • Zero Cloud Transmission: Your documents, photos, and media files never leave your device. We operate no external conversion servers. Nobody—including the developer—has access to your files.

3. Minimal Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Camera Permission: Used strictly in real-time by Apple’s native Document Scanner (VNDocumentCameraViewController) to scan documents. Camera streams are never recorded or analyzed for any other purpose.
  • Photo Library Access: Used strictly via Apple’s secure PhotosPicker to allow you to select media to convert. Mecofi only accesses the items you explicitly select.

4. No Third-Party Tracking & No Analytics SDKs

  • Mecofi contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs (e.g., no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Meta SDK).
  • In accordance with Apple’s Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), NSPrivacyTracking is set to false, and no tracking domains or collected data types are utilized.

5. In-App Purchases & Payment Security

  • All transactions are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2.
  • We never see, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details. Payment processing is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mecofi does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update our Privacy Policy if new features are added. However, our core commitment to zero data collection remains permanent. If you have any privacy-related inquiries, contact:
