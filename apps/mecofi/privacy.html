Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my lifetime Pro purchase?

If you have reinstalled Mecofi or switched to a new device:

  1. Open Mecofi.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (gear icon).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Restore Purchases”.

Your lifetime access will be validated immediately via Apple’s native StoreKit without any extra charge.

2. Do my files, photos, videos, or scans ever leave my device?

Never. Mecofi is engineered with a strict 100% on-device architecture. All document scans, image format conversions, video audio extractions, and PDF compilations are processed entirely locally on your iPhone using native Apple system frameworks (VisionKit, PDFKit, AVFoundation). Your files never touch external servers or cloud services.

3. Does Mecofi require an active internet connection?

No. All core features—document scanning, image conversions, media audio extractions, and PDF creation—operate completely offline. Internet access is only utilized by iOS when processing in-app purchases or restoring purchases through the Apple App Store.

4. What file formats does Mecofi support?

  • Documents & Scanning: Multi-page PDF creation, smart document scanning, auto-edge detection, and perspective correction.
  • Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF, GIF, BMP, and PDF.
  • Media & Audio: Video-to-audio extraction and conversion supporting MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, and CAF from MP4, MOV, and M4V video files.

5. Where are my converted files stored?

All processed files are stored in your device’s local Vault (sandboxed storage managed by SwiftData). You can preview, share, export to the iOS Files app, or delete them at any time. Deleting a file from the Vault or uninstalling the app permanently purges it from your device.

Privacy Policy

1. Zero Data Collection Pledge

Mecofi does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or monitor any personal data, device identifiers, conversion logs, documents, photos, or audio recordings. Your files belong exclusively to you.

2. 100% On-Device Processing

All computations are performed locally on your device’s hardware:

  • VisionKit & CoreImage: Powers on-device document detection and perspective correction.
  • PDFKit: Compiles photos and scans into PDFs locally.
  • AVFoundation & ImageIO: Executes media conversions and audio extractions natively.

No personal content or file data is ever transmitted to remote servers.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

Mecofi requests only the minimal iOS system permissions required to provide its core functionality:

  • Camera Permission: Used solely in real-time by the native Apple Document Scanner (VNDocumentCameraViewController) to capture physical documents. Camera streams are not recorded or analyzed for any other purpose.
  • Photo Library Access: Used strictly via Apple’s secure PhotosPicker to allow you to select images or videos you wish to convert or compile into PDFs. Mecofi only accesses the items you explicitly select.

4. Zero Third-Party SDKs & Zero Tracking

Mecofi contains no third-party tracking frameworks, no analytics beacons, and no advertising SDKs. We do not use cookies or behavioral tracking tools. Your activity within the app is completely private.

5. In-App Purchases & Financial Privacy

All financial transactions for Mecofi Pro are handled directly and securely by Apple through StoreKit 2. The developer does not receive, process, or store your credit card information, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mecofi does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe and adheres to global privacy standards, including COPPA and GDPR.

7. Changes to This Policy

Should we update our privacy practices, the latest revision will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date”.
