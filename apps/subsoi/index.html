Highlights

Smart Renewal Alerts

Customizable local reminders delivered in your selected app language—scheduled entirely on-device with no external push servers.

Detailed Spending Insights

Visualize monthly and annual recurring costs with native Swift Charts, categories, and clean multi-currency formatting.

Zero Data Collection

No account required, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking. Your financial records stay strictly on your iPhone via SwiftData.

Private iCloud Sync

Optionally sync across your Apple devices through your own end-to-end encrypted personal CloudKit container.

Face ID & Touch ID Lock

Keep your recurring expenses private with native biometric app lock powered by Apple’s LocalAuthentication.

Widgets & Free CSV Export

Track upcoming renewals right from your Home Screen and export your data to CSV anytime—free for all users.
