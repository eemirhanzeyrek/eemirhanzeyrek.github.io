Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Subsoi, navigate to Settings or the Upgrade to Pro screen, and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple StoreKit will validate your purchase immediately.

How do I manage or cancel my subscriptions?

Subsoi is a tracking and management tool designed to monitor your expenses. To cancel a third-party service, please open your device's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions or manage it through the respective service provider.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. No Data Collection: Subsoi does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, financial data, or tracking analytics.

2. On-Device Storage: All subscriptions, categories, payment records, and settings remain stored strictly on your local device via Apple SwiftData.

3. No Third-Party SDKs: We do not use third-party advertising, user tracking, or behavioral analytics frameworks.
