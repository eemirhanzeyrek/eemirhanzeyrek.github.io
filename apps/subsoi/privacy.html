Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does Subsoi Pro work?

Subsoi Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase (“Pay once, own forever”). There are no recurring subscription fees or auto-renewals for the app itself. Upgrading unlocks unlimited subscriptions, unlimited payment cards, customizable renewal reminders, Face ID / Touch ID app lock, private iCloud Sync, CSV data export, and Home Screen widgets.

2. How do I restore my Subsoi Pro purchase?

If you reinstall the app or set up a new Apple device:

  1. Open Subsoi and go to Settings (or tap the Subsoi Pro banner).
  2. Tap “Restore Purchases”.
  3. Apple StoreKit 2 will securely validate your previous purchase against your Apple ID and unlock your Pro access instantly.

3. How do I cancel or manage my third-party subscriptions?

Subsoi is a personal expense tracking, budgeting, and reminder tool. It does not directly charge or manage contracts with your service providers (such as Netflix, Spotify, iCloud, gym memberships, etc.).

  • To cancel an Apple-billed subscription, go to your iPhone Settings > [Your Name / Apple ID] > Subscriptions.
  • For direct third-party services, please log in to the respective provider’s website or app to cancel.

4. Where is my financial and subscription data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple’s native SwiftData framework. If you are a Pro user and choose to enable iCloud Sync, your data is encrypted and synced exclusively through your personal private Apple CloudKit container. We do not have servers, databases, or access to your financial records.

5. How do renewal reminders work?

Reminders are scheduled entirely on your local device using Apple’s UserNotifications framework. Subsoi does not use external push servers.

6. How can I export or permanently delete my data?

  • Export: Go to Settings > Data & Sync > Export Data (CSV) to generate a standard spreadsheet file.
  • Delete: Go to Settings > Data & Sync > Delete All Data to permanently erase all stored subscriptions, cards, and preferences from your device.

Privacy Policy

At Subsoi, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Subsoi is engineered from the ground up to operate with zero surveillance and zero data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection & No Account Required

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal, financial, or behavioral data.
  • You do not need to register, provide an email, or create an account to use Subsoi.
  • We have no knowledge of what services you subscribe to, the amounts you pay, or the payment methods you assign.

2. On-Device Storage & End-to-End Private iCloud Sync

  • Local Storage: All subscriptions, categories, currencies, and card records reside strictly on your device via Apple SwiftData.
  • iCloud Sync: If enabled, sync operations occur directly between your device and your personal Apple iCloud account (CloudKit Private Database). Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Nobody—including the developer—has access to your private iCloud data.

3. No Third-Party Tracking & No Analytics SDKs

  • Subsoi contains zero third-party advertising frameworks, tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs (e.g., no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK).
  • In accordance with Apple’s Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), NSPrivacyTracking is set to false, and no tracking domains or collected data types are utilized. Only standard local user defaults are accessed for app preferences (theme, currency, language).

4. In-App Purchases & Payment Security

  • All transactions are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2.
  • We never see, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details. Payment processing is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Biometric Security (Face ID / Touch ID)

  • If you enable Biometric App Lock, authentication is performed locally by Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework.
  • Subsoi never has access to your biometric data or biometric keys.

6. Children’s Privacy

Subsoi does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
